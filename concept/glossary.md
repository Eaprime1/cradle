# Glossary

- **point** — before the first step; a point to exist within.
- **black** — a space to exist in.
- **nullus** — the naught of I AM; a space to germinate into the project.
- **cradle** — the archetype: container of potential energy, originating
  environment, and end-to-end operational boundary.
- **aperture** — the gateway mechanism that mediates passage.
- **intake** — the process by which new concepts enter, operating in the first
  layer of the prime progression.
- **Penumbra** — Iteration 22; the half-shadow state between entry and decision.
- **adulteration** — entry without consent; see
  [the ethics framework](../ethics/first-adulteration.md).
