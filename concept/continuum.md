# Cradle Continuum

1. **SEED** — a possibility enters the boundary.
2. **CONTAIN** — the seed has a defined space in which to develop.
3. **SURPLUS** — development creates capacity beyond immediate need.
4. **ADMINISTER** — that capacity is organized and stewarded.
5. **SYMBOL** — experience becomes representable and shareable.
6. **STRAIN** — the boundary is tested by growth and accumulated demands.
7. **EXIT / TOMB** — the contents leave, or the boundary becomes their final record.

The continuum describes a lifecycle, not a requirement that every concept follow
one irreversible route.
