# Cradle Repository Guide

Cradle is the gestation node between **primordium** and **prime**, and the
standard conduit for passage between cosmological spaces:

`point → black → cradle-of-black → nullus → cradle-of-nullus → germination`

The archetype is a container of potential energy, an originating environment,
and an end-to-end operational boundary.

## Structure

- `concept/` defines the six-domain taxonomy, continuum, laws, and vocabulary.
- `domains/` contains the six application domains.
- `stages/`, `gateways/`, and `intake/` describe passage and concept entry.
- `ethics/` defines consent and adulteration.
- `iterations/` tracks development; Iteration 22 is **Penumbra**.
- `novella/` contains *The Sixth Cradle* materials.
- `timeline/` and `vault/` hold chronology and simulation materials.

## Invariants

- "Cradle" names the archetype; gateways use `cradle-of-<space>`.
- Consent must be affirmative, revocable, and gate-controlled.
- Consent-framework writing is precise and ethical, not shock content.
- Preserve figures, citations, and dates when editing domain or timeline material.
- Do not place anatomical anchor words in titles, filenames, labels, or UI strings.
