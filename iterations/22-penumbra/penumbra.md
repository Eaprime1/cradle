# Iteration 22 — Penumbra

> Stage: strain · Gateway: open · Consent: required

Penumbra is the half-shadow state between entry and decision. This iteration
examines what becomes visible after a crossing and before its meaning is settled.
Its work keeps the first-adulteration thread within a precise consent framework:
entry before consent, the persistence of its effects, and the responsibility
to stop ongoing harm and restore choice.
