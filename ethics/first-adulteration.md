# First Adulteration

Adulteration is entry before consent: the crossing occurs without an affirmative
decision or a real possibility of refusal. It changes the conditions of the
space before agreement can be made. The crossing is an unringable bell in the
sense that its occurrence cannot be erased; its effects continue until stopped.
The ethical response is to make the entry legible, halt continuing effects,
restore agency where possible, and prevent repetition. This is a consent
framework, not shock material.
