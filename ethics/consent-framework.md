# Consent Framework

Passage through a cradle requires all three conditions:

1. **Affirmative** — consent is an explicit yes, not silence or presumed
   willingness.
2. **Revocable** — consent can be withdrawn, and withdrawal stops or reverses
   passage where possible.
3. **Gate-controlled** — the gate remains closed unless consent is present and
   governs what may cross.

Without these conditions, entry is not consented passage.
