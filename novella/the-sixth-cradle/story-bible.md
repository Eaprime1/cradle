# Story Bible

*The Sixth Cradle* is speculative literary fiction. It reveals its pattern
through records, procedures, and structural echoes rather than direct summary.

## Continuity

- Mira Sallow is an archivist.
- Consent must be affirmative and revocable.
- The fact of entry and its continuing effects are distinct.
- The story's ethical focus is agency, record, and the possibility of exit.
