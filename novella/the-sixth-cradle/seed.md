# The Sixth Cradle — Seed

## Premise

Mira Sallow discovers a record that describes a passage no one remembers
accepting. The archive leaves her with a question it cannot answer: what can
still be repaired when the fact of entry cannot be undone?

## Protagonist

**Mira Sallow** — an archivist whose work is to preserve the conditions under
which a record can be trusted.

## Three tensions

1. A record can preserve an event without restoring what it changed.
2. A boundary can protect a space and also keep its occupants from leaving.
3. Refusing passage may be safer, while accepting it may be necessary for growth.
